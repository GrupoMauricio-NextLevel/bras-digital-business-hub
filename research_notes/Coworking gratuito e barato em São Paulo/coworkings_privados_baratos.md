# Low-cost private coworking spaces in São Paulo (centro / Brás / Mooca / Tatuapé / República / Liberdade + metro-connected), 2025–2026 prices

Research date: 2026-10-09. Most pricing pages show no publication date, so for those the "date" below is the date the page was accessed (2026-10-09). Several prices came only from search-engine snippets, and I could not open the page itself (Regus pages returned empty content and Woba pages returned 403). Those are marked **[snippet]** and should be treated as lower confidence.

## Q1. Chains with budget plans (WeWork, Regus/IWG, Gowork, Woba, etc.): current BRL prices

### Takeaway
The big chains are not cheap for a small team. WeWork's day pass in Centro is about R$ 214, and its cheapest coworking membership is about R$ 1,003–1,179/mo. Regus advertises "from" prices that are low per day (R$ 29–109 depending on product/unit) but leaves the final price on request. Gowork's cheapest published item is a fiscal address from R$ 79/mo, with private rooms at R$ 900 per station. None of the chains I checked has a unit in Brás, Mooca, Pari or Belém. Their "Tatuapé" and "Liberdade" landing pages actually point to Paulista-corridor units.

## Q3. Virtual office / endereço fiscal for opening a CNPJ in São Paulo: prices, legal caveats, cheapest reputable providers

### Takeaway
Published fiscal-address prices in SP run from about R$ 50 to R$ 150/mo. Examples: Coworking Higienópolis R$ 81/mo for a service-company fiscal address on the annual plan; Gowork from R$ 79/mo; contabilidade.com R$ 50 bundled with accounting or R$ 150 standalone; Myhub commercial address +R$ 50. The main legal risk in São Paulo is municipal viability (zoning plus CNAE compatibility). In addition, Lei Municipal 16.757/2017 makes coworkings and virtual offices jointly liable for the ISS of clients without a CCM registration. For trading (ICMS) activities, you need a plan that supports Inscrição Estadual.

### Cited Findings
**Prices**
- Coworking Higienópolis: Endereço Comercial R$ 50/mo (annual, R$ 900 by boleto) or R$ 80/mo by card. Endereço Fiscal de Serviços R$ 81/mo (annual, R$ 972 by boleto) or R$ 120/mo by card; it includes use of the address in a service company's articles of incorporation — [Coworking Centro SP](https://coworkinghigienopolis.com/coworking-centro-sp/)
- Gowork: endereço fiscal from R$ 79/mo (Paulista/Al. Santos units) — [Gowork Liberdade](https://www.gowork.com.br/coworking-na-liberdade/)
- contabilidade.com: virtual office R$ 50/mo with an accounting plan, or R$ 150/mo standalone (article published 08 Apr 2026) — [contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/escritorio-virtual-em-sao-paulo-o-que-verificar-antes-de-contratar/)
- Contabilizei (updated 01 Oct 2025): virtual office costs range "de R$ 19 a mais de R$ 300 por mês" in Brazil. Contabilizei bundles a virtual office into its monthly fee for clients but gives no figure — [Contabilizei](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/contabilidade-online/escritorio-virtual/)
- Myhub: commercial address +R$ 50/mo on the Focus plan, included in Pro (R$ 550) — [Myhub preços](https://www.myhub.com.br/coworking-sp-preco/)
- CompanyHero SP units: Av. Paulista, Faria Lima, Berrini, Vila Leopoldina, Chácara Sto. Antônio, Alphaville, Guarulhos and **Tatuapé**. Includes fiscal and commercial address, unlimited mail and 20% off meeting rooms. Prices were not visible on the fetched page (annual boleto / annual installments / monthly options) — [CompanyHero unidades SP](https://www.companyhero.com/escritorio-virtual/unidades-sao-paulo)
- CompanyHero offers a specific "escritório virtual com Inscrição Estadual" plan for e-commerce/trading at Av. Paulista and Berrini, price on request — [CompanyHero IE Paulista](https://www.companyhero.com/escritorio-virtual-inscricao-estadual/av-paulista)
- **[snippet]** Elos Coworking: virtual office in Zona Norte for R$ 99 with fiscal and commercial address; exact address not given — [OHub listing](https://www.ohub.com.br/anuncio/endereao-fiscal-63141)

**Legal caveats**
- The contabilidade.com article (Apr 2026) calls municipal viability "o ponto mais crítico": "Escolher o endereço errado pode travar a abertura do CNPJ ou gerar problemas com a prefeitura". It adds that SP has "regras rigorosas de zoneamento urbano e fiscalização municipal", that "nem toda atividade pode usar endereço virtual" (check the CNAE), and that a residential address may sometimes be a better choice — [contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/escritorio-virtual-em-sao-paulo-o-que-verificar-antes-de-contratar/)
- For service providers, a fiscal address can be used without a commercial premises. The fiscal address appears on the cartão CNPJ and alvarás and receives official correspondence — [Contabilizei](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/contabilidade-online/escritorio-virtual/)
- Lei Municipal SP 16.757/2017 amended Lei 13.701/2003 (art. 13) to make coworkings and virtual offices jointly responsible for the ISS of clients not registered in the CCM. In practice, providers therefore require clients to have a CCM. One lawyer argues the rule is illegal/unconstitutional; that is an opinion, not a court ruling — [Legisweb](https://www.legisweb.com.br/noticia/?id=20062); [COAD](https://www.coad.com.br/home/noticias-detalhe/84886/lei-municipal-de-sp-obriga-coworkings-a-cobrar-iss-de-seus-clientes); [Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/?p=444984)
- Companies selling goods (ICMS) need an address that accepts Inscrição Estadual, which only some plans offer — [CompanyHero IE Paulista](https://www.companyhero.com/escritorio-virtual-inscricao-estadual/av-paulista)
- **[snippet]** The alphanumeric CNPJ started being issued on 31 Jul 2026 for new companies only; existing companies do not need to change — [CompanyHero blog](https://www.companyhero.com/blog/tag/principal-ev)

### Inferences
- The cheapest credible route for a service startup is about R$ 50–81/mo (Higienópolis or Gowork, paid annually by boleto) or a bundle with an online accountant (about R$ 50 on top of the accounting fee). The startup must obtain its own CCM so the provider accepts it under Lei 16.757/2017.
- A Brás-area textile/commerce business needing IE should expect to pay more and must specifically ask for an IE-compatible plan.

### Gaps
- I found no official Prefeitura de SP page (SF/SMUL) confirming the current viability and IPTU rules for virtual offices. I could not confirm whether Lei 16.757/2017 is still in force unchanged.
- CompanyHero's actual BRL prices were not visible.
- I found no fiscal-address provider physically in Brás, Pari or Belém with a published price.
